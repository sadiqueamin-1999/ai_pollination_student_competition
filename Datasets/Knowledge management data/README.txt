#Summary of data

## 1 - single document

A single document including text, figures and tables

## 2 - multiple documents

Multiple travel policy documents from Cardiff University. Some of these documents are in Welsh if you'd like the additional challenge of handling documents in multiple languages (you will not score lower for not handing documents in Welsh).

## 3 - example festival documents

A series of documents made to simulate a festival. A summary of the documents included are as follows:

- `Festival_Documents/`: 20 documents in PDF and DOCX, grouped by category: customer pack, staff pack, policy, safety and licensing, logistics and supply, commercial and financial, people, reports. Mixed text, tables and figures.
- `Crew_Onboarding/`
  - `00_Templates/`: crew agreement template (with tick-box offer options), offer options guide, crew list template.
  - `01_Standard_Documents/`: crew terms and conditions, crew welcome pack.
  - `02_crew_agreements/`: nine crew members the festival has hired or otherwise providing a service at the festival. Each crew member has one folder. Each folder contains an agreement, info pack, crew list and invoice or expense claim.
  
These documents contain a number of known errors in the crew agreements, for example:
 - unsigned documents
 - inconsistent figures
 
**Note:** The festival documents are AI generated and do not represent a real festival.
